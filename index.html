    A NOTE ON THIS ATLAS

    A board, not a survey.

    This is an unofficial fan interpretation of Tom’s Crossing by Mark Z. Danielewski. It follows characters and chapters so you can explore alongside your own reading.

    The carved hills, vegetation, buildings, distances, and bends are imagined scenery. Gold lines show the order of a journey, not a surveyed trail. Journal notes explain where the geography is uncertain.

    The atlas is being built as one reader listens to the audiobook. Character and place spellings follow the supplied reading notes, derived from audio rather than printed text, and remain provisional.

    The atlas covers disclosed movements through part of Chapter Twelve. The final chapter is partial. Reading limits reset on reload; select only a chapter you have reached. Historical accounts, plans, dreams and spectral events have separate journal layers. A last observation does not imply continuous presence afterward.

    Drag to orbit, Shift-drag or right-drag to pan, and scroll to zoom. On touch screens, drag with one finger to orbit and use two fingers to pan or zoom. Select a journal entry to move the character’s piece.
